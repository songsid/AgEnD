# M-3533 R1b: room office styles and Studio (large) (real app)

These are captures of the real Hub and its real pages from this branch, taken headless with Chromium. The room is the R1 one: 12 people and 30 agents of 6 owners, plus the room assistant, with made-up names. Two agents are working elsewhere and one is paused.

The comparison captures are taken with **motion reduced**, the viewer's own setting, so everyone is on their seat. That shows the layout itself. One capture with **ordinary motion** shows the real thing: an idle agent now and then strolls near its seat and back, as in the Fleet office, so a tag may be covered for a moment. How strolling figures avoid tags is the Fleet drawing's own work (M-3539), not special-cased for rooms.

The room's office has no options row over the scene (the design's r3: the office as a tab, no extra row). Its options (hide paused, bedtime, dialogue, the key) open from the gear at the bottom of the zoom stack on a desk, and from the room's ⋯ → 辦公室選項 on a phone. A shared page's seats now restart when the style changes, so a room switching from the Studio to Studio (large) is drawn with everyone on its new seats, not on the Studio's overflow spots.

Seat counts are read from each style's layout, never typed. A style's total is every seat of its four areas, the 9 spots of 求救區 (where an agent that needs you goes; no chairs are drawn there) included: Studio (large) is 12 desks + 30 to rest + 9 for help + 6 to nap = 57, the Studio 12 + 14 + 9 + 6 = 41. The room assistant is a figure in the office like the agents (31 figures for "30 個 Agent · 房間助理"), and the hint names it when the room has one, so the hint, the header and the phone's area chips count the same people. Cozy's 14 is its whole layout: 6 desks, 3 to rest, 2 for help and 3 for nap. Studio (large) is offered only in a room's settings; the Fleet office's own style picker is unchanged.
