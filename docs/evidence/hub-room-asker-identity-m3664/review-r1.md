6. **Key change.** The old pinned issuer/encryption binding may read its historical records only within their original retention (up to24h). A new pairing/key selects a different binding/store and does not adopt the old records or revive a turn.
7. **Assistant classes.** The agreed three-kind union remains intact. `room/<id>/assistant`, `room/<id>/poll` and `application/<id>/principal/<principal>` distinguish the non-person classes. The consumer may use the stable prefix for presentation; every class remains assistant, never human authority. This distinction is explicitly included in the consumer design/update.
8. **Agent label.** The source comment documents `via_name` as the immutable agent label for an on-behalf projection, not the displayed person's name.
