# Confirmed group-policy control

This internal control completes the server-side preview/apply contract for [#3298](https://github.com/songsid/AgEnD/issues/3298). It is deliberately not composed: actual pairing and publication enrollment must be enforced before an enable route or capability mark is exposed. The HTTP layer will still supply its existing configured-admin, same-origin and CSRF gates. There is no additional fleet confirmation dialog.

The control itself authenticates the configured immutable directory administrator before work and after directory awaits, retaining the same session and request/current fences. Group selections are re-resolved at both preview and apply: the server's current GUID must match the selected identity, and the saved DN is the server's form. Recreating a group at an old DN cannot inherit the selection.

The inventory derives people, active credential-bearing fleets, published unfrozen exact agent revisions, active applicable ask grants and lifecycle rows from the same Hub DB. It never enumerates AD or manufactures a person. Revoked, withdrawn, replaced or frozen resources cannot be captured or resumed. Person labels are display only; owner/material/grant/lifecycle fingerprints fence the confirmation. Routine eligibility-cache timestamps do not invalidate it.

Preview is read-only. Its short-lived, single-use server candidate binds the session, requested and canonical policy, complete prior policy, global authorization version, inventory, semantic fresh group facts and displayed impact. The outward object shares no mutable state with it. Apply consumes the candidate and obtains all facts and administrator authority again. A stale revision, resource, session, group identity, fact or impact refuses the save and requires a new preview. Changed membership evidence is audited without authority writes, including unknown becoming known. Fresh observation timestamps alone do not change semantic facts.

Unknown people remain a separate HELD group and do not prevent saving a stable confirmed policy. They gain no new group access, and an outage does not suspend their existing resources. The server's exact pre-enable inventory may be confirmed as manual revisions; it never grandfathers askers or later revisions. Explicit person denies and switching group-derived permissions off remain confirmed narrowing. Suspended resources require the separate explicit resume action even after membership recovers or policy is switched back on.

One immediate outer transaction captures exact manual provenance under the configured administrator, replaces the policy, suspends the exact planned rows and records required hash-bound observation/impact evidence. Every write and audit shares that DB and transaction. Material/lifecycle/policy/session fences are checked again after audit. A meaningful authority change advances the global transport version exactly once in that transaction; sockets refresh only after commit. A confirmed unchanged save records fresh impact evidence without changing policy revision or reconnecting fleets. Missing, asynchronous, throwing or reentrant writers withhold the result and roll back authority changes. As with the existing transport store, a monotonic-floor advance followed by transaction failure may freeze state as uncertain; the control never resets that floor.

Tests use real scratch SQLite, policy/resource/transport libraries and hash-chain audit, with fake people and inert directory/session callbacks. Existing fake-LDAPS wire tests exercise the driver separately; they do not make the control a real-directory or composed-HTTP acceptance. No editor, browser, real account, pilot or native-host operation is part of this step. There is no new schema. Rollback is a code revert while the control remains unmounted.

The preview deadline is a commit boundary, not only an entry check: apply rechecks both clock bounds after preparation and inside the transaction, including after required audit/version work. Expiry during a held administrator lookup or reentrant audit rolls back the pending change. Its consumed candidate stays consumed.
