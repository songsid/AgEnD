- **Web: Edit offices opens on the right.** On a screen 1200 px or wider, the Office editor docks on the right, where the AI usage rail is, instead of opening as a dialog over the page. The Office stays in view and usable beside it, and the usage rail comes back when the editor closes. Close it with ×, Esc, Cancel or Save; focus returns to Edit offices. Other settings-like editors can open in the same place later. Narrower screens keep the centred dialog. (#2585)
