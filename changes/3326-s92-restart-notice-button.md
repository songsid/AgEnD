section: Changed
---
- **Restart AgEnD from the restart notice, even after a hand edit, and on web-only fleets.** When fleet.yaml was changed by hand, the "take effect after AgEnD restarts" notice now has a Restart now button, the same as after a Save, instead of telling you to run a command on the host. On a fleet with no chat app, a restart from Settings now works too. It is recorded as a dashboard notice where the chat announcement would go, with every other safeguard unchanged.
