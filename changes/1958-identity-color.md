- **Pick each agent's colour, and where it shows (#1958).**
  - **Changing it:** in the web dashboard, **⋯ → Colour…** (or View's **Edit profile**). Pick one of 12 colours or your own, and choose whether it shows on the agent's name, a left bar or the ring around its picture. **Reset** goes back.
  - **Agents** can set their own with `set_web_theme` (`identity`), and a person's pick always wins.
  - **Automatic colours:** an agent nobody chose for gets one of a few soft automatic colours.
  - **Settings:** the tab is now **Settings → Web appearance**. It also sets where colours show by default, and has **Turn off colours** and **Restore defaults**.
