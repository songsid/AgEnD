- Phone chat: one thin line above the message box now shows the model, its effort and how much of the context is used ("Opus 5.5 · high · 56%"). Tap it to change the model. The box itself is just 📎, the text across its whole width, and Send or Stop. Attached files sit in a strip at the top, and a long message stops at five lines and scrolls. The working bar and the line under the box are gone on phones. Desktop is unchanged.
