- **Settings → Accounts: "Cancel sign-in and remove" (#3212).** Remove was refused with nothing to do about it while the account's sign-in was running. After that refusal the dialog now offers **Cancel sign-in and remove**, which cancels the running sign-in and then removes the account (the same request, the same typed name). It is offered only for an account on this host, and only after that refusal.
