- **The task board for agents working together (#2303, step 2 of #2246).** Instances hear about their cards: a `[Task board]`
  notice from AgEnD when someone else assigns one (once it can start), starts it, sends it back, answers its blocked
  question, comments, finishes the last card it waited for, confirms it or cancels it — 10 s of news in one notice, a
  whole plan assigned at once as a count, held by the delivery outbox until the instance can take it. A card records
  who handed it out (its **dispatcher**): a blocked question goes to that instance first, which asks you and answers on
  the card. An instance works on one card at a time (cards it opened for itself do not count; `board_cards_at_once` raises
  the limit for a role such as a reviewer). A dispatcher's answer to a card whose assignee is busy is kept on the card
  until the assignee resumes it. The `task` tool's own tasks are not announced (they come with their own message); a
  notice leaves out what can no longer be acted on, and a comment its assignee has already read. Over the public link,
  a visible card no longer shows hidden instances' related cards, comments or history. A main task that reaches you before it is wrapped up says
  **To fill in**, its owner is told, and Confirm asks first; a main task's tile shows who works on it now. See
  [features](features.md#on-the-dashboard-fleet--tasks).
