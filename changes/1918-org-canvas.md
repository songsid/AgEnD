section: Changed
---
- **Web: Fleet → Org chart is now a canvas you can pan and zoom (#1918).** General sits on top, then the teams in `fleet.yaml`'s order in boxes, and the instances in no team last. Each card shows the avatar, the identity colour, the state and the backend; clicking it opens that instance's chat.
  - **Moving around:** drag to pan, use the wheel or pinch to zoom, or press + / − / 0. The overview in the corner jumps to where you click.
  - **Opening view:** the chart opens on General and the first row of teams. On a phone, teams stack one a row so the cards stay readable. 0, or the fit button, shows the whole fleet.
  - **Folding:** clicking a team's header folds it to a count of its members by state. No other team moves, and folds are kept on this device.
  - **Search:** Find dims everything else and counts the matches, and the overview marks where they are. Enter opens the first match's team and brings that card into view.
  - **List:** a switch beside the search gives the previous list layout, also kept on this device. With the keyboard, Tab and the arrow keys move between cards and Enter opens a chat.
  - Nothing is edited here; Fleet → Teams is still where teams change.
