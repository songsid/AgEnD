- **Hub wording (#3700):** the Hub and a Fleet's Settings → Hub now use one set of words. A Fleet "connects" to a Hub, a connection is removed or reconnected, and agents are "shared". You sign in with your "company account", and the person who runs the Hub is the "Hub administrator". "Pair" and "pairing code" appear only on the pairing-code path, under Other ways.
