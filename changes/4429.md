section: Changed
---
- **The Traditional Chinese interface uses one word per concept.** The English words left inside zh-TW messages and pages now follow the approved glossary: an instance is an Agent, a session 工作階段, a backend 後端, Context 上下文, a tunnel 通道 (固定通道 / 臨時通道), a topic 主題, a pane 窗格, a team 團隊 and a persona 人設. Config keys, commands, paths and quoted button text are unchanged. A test now fails on the three known wrong forms (the retired word for admin, the Japanese form of 覽 and the variant form of 佔用) anywhere in the source or docs.
