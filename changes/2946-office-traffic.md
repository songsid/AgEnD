section: Changed

- **Office traffic is easier to see (#2946, reported by hxhsandy).** The items carried between desks (a task's folder, a message's envelope, a report's paper, a broadcast's horn) are drawn 1.75× larger and grow with the room as its people do. Each now stays in the air for at least 2.5 seconds. A burst no longer builds a backlog that lags behind real events: at most 12 fly at once, newest first, and one already in the air is never cut short. A new **Key** button in the Office bar shows the four shapes and what they mean. Office styles from a skin pack keep their own items. With reduced motion, nothing flies, as before.
