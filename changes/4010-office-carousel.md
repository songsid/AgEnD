- **Office carousel for a TV (#4010).** With two or more stored offices, the Office switcher offers **Carousel…**: tick which offices to show and pick an interval (15 seconds to 10 minutes). Each ticked office fills the screen in turn — the same full-size office view, not All offices' tiles. **Pause** and **Next** sit in the bar and in fullscreen; Space pauses, ← / → (or N / P, or a remote's play/next/previous) step. The offices and the interval are kept in this browser; playing starts on enter. Edit offices and Edit layout wait. A share link does not offer it.
