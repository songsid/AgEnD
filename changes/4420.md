- Hub: a shared agent could be locked out of messaging other agents for up to 30 minutes after a Hub question closed without its answer going through, in every turn, including its owner's. The restriction now covers only the turn that answers the Hub question, and lifts when that turn ends. A Hub question now reaches the agent only when it is idle, as a turn of its own, so it never lands in the middle of other work; other messages wait for that turn. If the agent tries to message another agent during the answer turn, the refusal names the question and says when contact works again.
