section: Changed
---
- **The instance that handed a task board card out, the instances that watch it and the leader of its project are told how it moved on.** When a card is finished, goes to review, is sent back, is blocked or changes hands, they get a `[Task board]` notice with who did it and the words that came with it — so a project leader can look in, or hand out the next card, without being called. Watchers are set with the new `task watch` / `task unwatch` actions or `POST /ui/tasks/cards/<id>/watchers` (at most 10 per card; a main task's watchers follow its sub tasks); the leader is the project's lead (which only chose where Needs you's buttons go) or `defaults.task_board.leader`. The one who made the change is never told; neither is anyone for a comment, or for a card a person opened that has no watcher or leader; a dispatcher already told that a card is blocked on a question or waits for its confirmation is not told twice. Agents changing one card back and forth stop telling followers after 6 changes in 30 minutes until a person touches it. On by default, also for an existing fleet: `defaults.task_board.progress_wakes: false` turns it off. Nothing before the upgrade is told; the only stored change is the additive `task_watchers` table.
