- **Hub Administration: 「Let colleagues join」 in three steps (#3698).** The first card on Administration shows three steps, each marked done from what the Hub has saved:
  1. Switch on company-account connections.
  2. Choose who can join. Find an AD group by the start of its name, set how many Fleets each person may connect, and save once; that also turns the group permissions on. A live join code also counts as done. A group the search can't find can still be added by its distinguished name.
  3. Copy one sentence for colleagues: "In AgEnD, open Settings → Hub, enter {host}, then sign in with the company account." It unlocks once steps 1 and 2 are done.
- **The long company link** is now under "Link with key fingerprint (Advanced)", for anyone who must check the fingerprint by hand. Individual invitations are folded under the card.
- **The welcome page:** an administrator whose Hub has no connected Fleet yet sees the same card instead of "Your Hub admin pairs fleets for you". Someone without a Fleet reads the step 3 sentence, and connecting with a pairing code moves under "Other ways".
- **The fleet list** names each Fleet by its label, with the owner below. Revoked Fleets are folded under "Revoked (n)".
