section: Changed
---
- On a phone, the button at the top left now follows one rule. ☰ opens the agent list, ← goes back one level (from an agent's pages to the list, or from a Settings section to Settings), and ✕ closes the agent list. The agent list used to close with ←, and a Settings section had ☰ with a separate "‹ Settings" bar under it.
