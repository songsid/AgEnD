- **A task card's page lists its watchers and its leader, and its timeline shows when watchers changed.** In the property column, under **Dispatched by**: **Watchers** shows the instances that hear this card's progress, each with a ✕ to stop it, and **Add** offers the fleet's other instances (one with the task board off is greyed with the reason; a card has at most 10, and then Add says so). A sub task also shows its main task's watchers. **Leader** names who leads the card — the project's lead, or the board's default — and says so when it would hear nothing (it has the card itself, left the fleet, its task board is off, or progress notices are off). A change of watchers is a line of the timeline ("Ann changed watchers: +ops, −dev-b", under **History** too). A refusal shows in the page's own words. Nothing is drawn for a guest or a read-only public link. The card's read gains `leader`, `inheritedWatchers` and `followsProgress` (additive; nothing stored changes, and the public link names no hidden instance in them).
