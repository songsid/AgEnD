section: Changed
---
- **A card's page fills the content area, like the board.** The card page was held to the Fleet page's 1240 px, so on a wide screen it sat in the middle with empty sides. It now has no maximum width: the properties column grows with the window (300 px on a 1366 px screen, up to 460 px), the label column grows from 7rem to 10rem, and the main column takes the rest. A line of prose (a field's text, a comment's words) still stops at 72rem, so a long text never becomes one line across a 2560 px screen; the frames, the checklist, the sub tasks and the activity cards use the full width. At 1366 px and on a phone the page is as before; nothing scrolls sideways at 1366, 1920, 2560 or 390 px, light or dark.
