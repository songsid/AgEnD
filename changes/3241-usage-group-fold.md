- AI subscription usage, in the grouped view: a provider with two or more accounts (such as Claude ×5) now has a −/+ button at the right of its header. It cycles through every window of each account, then one line per account with only its fullest window (bar, percentage and reset), then just the header with the group's highest percentage. Warning colours stay in every step, and this browser remembers the choice for each provider. This works in the side panel and the Usage window, on a computer and a phone.
