- A Hub room's office can take a style: in Room settings → Room office, a room manager picks Studio (large), Studio, Cozy, Guild or School, each shown with how many seats it has, and saves it; the room's chat says who changed it. Studio (large) is new, for big rooms: the same desks as the Studio and a rest area large enough that 30 agents each have a seat. When a room has more agents than its style has seats to rest in, the settings suggest a larger style.
