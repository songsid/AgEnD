- **Web Settings: Preferences and Web appearance save with Save too (#2347).** A theme, language, colour or card-size choice is shown at once and counted as **Your preferences** in the Settings bar. Switches kept on this device only (following reduced motion, custom CSS off here) count on their own. **Save** keeps them, on every device you sign in to (or in this browser only over a temporary public link). **Discard**, or leaving Settings, puts them back. The **Experimental: Canvas Worker effects** switch is saved with the other fleet changes. **Restore defaults** is a confirmed action and still saves at once.
