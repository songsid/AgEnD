- AI subscription usage: an account that no agent uses no longer has "No agents on this profile" written beside its name. A small, quiet ⓘ sits there instead. Its words show when you point at it, move to it with Tab or tap it, and screen readers read them. Tapping the ⓘ on a phone shows the words without opening the account. This works in the side panel and the Usage window, in every view.
