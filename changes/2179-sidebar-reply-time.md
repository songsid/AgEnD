- **The sidebar shows who has replied (#2179).** Each row shows when that agent last replied to a person (its reply tool, on a channel or in the web chat; cross-instance messages don't count). The name and context % are on the first line, and the time ("just now", "2 min ago", "3 h ago") is at the end of the second.
  - **Highlight:** a reply under 10 minutes old is highlighted, and the highlight fades by itself. There are no unread markers to clear.
  - **Sorting:** a sort button next to **Edit** says how the list is sorted. **Custom**, the default, keeps your own order, and a reply never moves a row. **Recent replies** lists the rest by last reply, without groups. Your favorites stay pinned in your order, or, if you choose, are sorted by last reply too. The choice follows you across devices.
  - **Guests** see no reply times, and the anonymous View page (which has none) always lists in your Custom order.
