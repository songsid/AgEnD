- **Office: each resident can have their own lines (#3681).** A skin pack's speech-bubble lines no longer have to be shared by everyone. A character set may carry its own `bubbles` beside its poses, and `bubblesByInstance` gives one instance its own lines — the same shape and limits as the pack's `bubbles` (up to 12 lines a state, 1–40 characters each). A person says their own lines first, then their character set's, then the pack's, then the page's. Packs without them draw as before; an Office share link keeps only the lines of the people it shows.
