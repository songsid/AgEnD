- **Retention for delivery-outbox.db and the Task Board (#1335).** Both stores grew without bound: `delivery-outbox.db` was accumulating ~2.5 MB/day and the Task Board had 1,099 rows going back to April. Terminal deliveries (`delivered`/`failed`) and `done`/`cancelled` tasks older than `defaults.retention_days` (default 30) are now pruned once at fleet startup and daily after that. `uncertain` rows and non-terminal deliveries are never pruned. Deliveries with still-open reply obligations are skipped until the obligation is closed. Cancelled task prerequisites of live tasks are also kept. Pruning runs in chunks of at most 500 rows and yields between chunks so it never stalls the event loop. When a `delivery_status` query returns no results for a `delivery_id`, the fleet now checks whether the row was pruned and responds with "older than the retention window; record pruned" — but only to the original source or target of that delivery — rather than "Delivery not found", preserving the protocol invariant that "not found" means "never delivered". The unfiltered `task list` is now capped at 100 rows (most recently updated first) with a note about how many were omitted; filtered lists are uncapped.
