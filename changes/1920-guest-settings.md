- **Settings → Web → Guests (#1920).** Guest sign-in for people who aren't admins is now set up from the dashboard:
  - **Switches:** turn guest sign-in on or off, and let guests chat or keep them read-only.
  - **Open connections:** for a connection open to everyone, tick the agents its guests may reach.
  - **Guest accounts:** see each one, with their platform, how many agents they reach, their sessions, remembered browsers and when they were last seen.
  - **Narrow or sign out:** narrow a guest to some of the agents they can reach (only those are offered), or sign one out at once.
  - **Confirmation:** a change that lets guests reach more waits for a fleet admin's confirmation, as other access changes do. A change that only takes away applies at once.
