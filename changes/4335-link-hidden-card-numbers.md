- **Over the public link, a card kept out of sight is no longer numbered by what points at it.** A sub task of a main task kept out of sight no longer reads the main task's number (its `parentId`, and the `parent` of its "created" event, read as none), and the number of a hidden card could also be read from a visible card's `dependsOn` and `unmetDeps` (the card's read, its sub tasks', the board's, the work drawer's and the search's), from an "updated" event's `changes.dependsOn` in its history, and from the `cascade` / `restored` of a sub task's "moved" event. Those numbers are now left out (an "updated" event with nothing visible left is not shown). The dashboard and admin sessions still see every number. A card that waits for a card kept out of sight still shows as waiting: over the link its read carries `hiddenUnmet` (how many unfinished dependencies were left out), and the page shows the clock and "waits for a card that is not public" instead of an empty list.
