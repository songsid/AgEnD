section: Changed
---
- Over the public link (temporary or fixed), a fleet admin can now change what they could only change on the dashboard reached directly:
  - the task board, schedules and teams;
  - the Office editor and agents' themes;
  - preferences, favourites and pins;
  - Settings → Web, Access & security (including Guests and Web authority) and Look's limits;
  - Retry now and Close public link.
- A change that opens a link can be confirmed over the link too, as the settings role allows.
- The usage report is offered there by default, and attachments play in the page.
- Guests are unchanged. A read-only session can no longer change the task board anywhere.
