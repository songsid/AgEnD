- Web chat, HTML preview panel: when the page reports JavaScript errors (uncaught errors, unhandled rejections, `console.error`), an **errors chip** with their count appears in the panel's bar. Clicking it puts a reply about the page in the message box with the errors quoted under a line saying the text comes from the page, for you to check, edit and send. At most five are quoted, and the rest are counted. Nothing is sent until you send it, and Reload starts a fresh count.
