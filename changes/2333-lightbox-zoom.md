- **Web chat: zoom and pan in the image viewer (#2333).** A big picture (a 1500×5581 before/after sheet opened at about 15%) could not be zoomed to read its detail. Now the viewer zooms with the wheel around the pointer (the wheel scrolls once the picture is larger than the window, and Ctrl/⌘+wheel or a trackpad pinch zooms). A click toggles fit ↔ 100% at that spot, and a drag pans. **Fit width** reads a tall picture by scrolling. The toolbar shows the zoom level between − and +, and the keys are `+`, `-`, `0`, `1` and `W`. On a phone: pinch, a one-finger drag, and a double tap for 100%. Smooth (a transform, no re-decode), and it works on the public link too.
