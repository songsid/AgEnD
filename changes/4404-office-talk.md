- **Talk to an agent from the Office.** **Talk** in the Office's ⚙ Display options (per browser, dashboard only) picks what **Chat** does there: the pop-up window as before (default), a side panel beside the map (a bottom sheet on a phone), a visual-novel dialogue box laid over the foot of the scene, or the reply drawn over the agent's head. All four show the same conversation as the chat, and Enter sends.
- The dialogue box shows only the agent's side: the whole reply, typed out a page at a time, with a large portrait; a click shows the rest, the next page, or closes it. **History** holds both sides and opens the full chat. Reading is **Click** (default), **Auto** or **Fast**, and **Skip** closes every reply; the head bubble reads the same way.
- A skin pack may style the reply over the head with `dialogBox.bubble` (colours, a 9-slice frame, and `enter`: pop, fade or none).
- **All offices is the Office's home map.** With two or more offices stored, the Office opens on All offices until you pick another in the switcher; a click on a tile opens that office for this visit, and **← All offices** in the bar goes back.
