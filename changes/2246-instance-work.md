- **Who works on what on the task board (#2246).** The board's **Instances** button opens a drawer that lists every instance by team, working ones first. Each instance shows its cards as its `task mine` reads them: in progress, blocked, waiting for you, the main tasks it coordinates, what it can start, and what waits for other cards. A card opens from there. An instance's Details page shows its own cards too. Over the public link, instances kept out of sight are not listed.
