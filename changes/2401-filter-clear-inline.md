- **Sidebar filter: setting a Status or CLI facet no longer makes the filter panel taller (#2401).** Clear and the "shown" after the count used to widen the row. Then the CLI header and Clear wrapped onto a row of their own, pushing the search box up; a folded group's summary could do the same. The count, "shown" and Clear now stack at the row's right end beside an open list, in a column as wide with a facet set as without. With every group folded, Clear takes the count's place on the same line, and the summary uses only the room its line has left. The panel keeps its height when a facet is set or cleared, open or folded, on desktop and phone.
