- A Hub room has an office: next to Chat, the Office tab draws the room's agents and its assistant in a shared office scene, each in the same state the member list shows, and marks the ones answering a question asked in this room. Press an agent to see its owner and introduction. On a phone, Office is the first item of the room's ⋯ menu. A room manager can turn it off in Room settings → Room office; it is on for every room, and the room's chat says when a manager turns it on or off.
