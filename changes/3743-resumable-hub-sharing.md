- Hub owners can now pause and resume sharing without clearing the people and AD groups allowed to ask. Paused agents disappear from new-chat choices; existing authorized chats show the pause and stay readable. Original grant expiry dates keep running, including retained longer grants. Resume cannot restore expired/revoked grants or undo a Hub policy hold. Stop sharing remains a separate permanent action. Pauses survive Fleet reconnects and Hub restarts, both actions are audited, and old answer-release leases are retired immediately. A pre-pause authorization already travelling to the Fleet may still start one input within its existing five-second lease; after the member observes the authorization refresh, it stays retired even after Resume. Pause is not instant cancellation of running work.
