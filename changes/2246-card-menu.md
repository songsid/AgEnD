- **A card's menu on the task board, and the next card.** Right-click a card (a tile, a row waiting for you), or press the menu key (Shift+F10) on it, for its menu: open it; confirm, send back or answer it when it waits for you; move it to another status; change its assignee or priority; copy its link or number; cancel it. Each choice asks what the card's dialog asks. Shift+right-click keeps the browser's menu. An open card's arrows, or **K** / **J**, step to the previous / next card as the page showed them, so you can go through the cards waiting for you one after another. See [features](features.md#on-the-dashboard-fleet--tasks).
