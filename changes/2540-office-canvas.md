section: Changed
---
- The Office view fills the Fleet panel and pans and zooms like the org chart's Canvas: drag to pan, the wheel or a pinch to zoom, **+**/**−**/**Fit**/**Fill** buttons (the zoom shown as a percentage) or the `+ - 0` keys, and an overview to jump (not on a phone). It takes the Fleet area's whole width, down to the window's foot. **Fit** shows the whole scene as large as the panel allows; **Fill** (kept per office) fills the panel, cropping the overflow (drag to see it). **+**/**−** step in pixel levels, and drawing is always unsmoothed. A resize fits a fitted view again and the scene can never be dragged off the panel. The view and Fit/Fill are kept per device for each office and style. The whole panel around the 16:9 scene is a matte — a skin pack's new optional `matte` field (`#rrggbb`, checked by the page and the host), else the background's edge colour, else the skin's own (Studio's hall floor; Desks: the dashboard's background). Hover, click, name tags, bubbles, crowd counts and **Edit layout** work at any zoom (a drag on a handle moves it, anywhere else pans).
- Skin packs (format v1) gain an optional `foreground`: a 16:9 PNG over the whole zones scene, drawn above the people, furniture and items in the air but below name tags, bubbles and crowd counts — for door frames, pillars and table fronts that hide whoever walks behind them. A furniture item's `depth` may now be `"y"`, drawing it among the people by base y (whoever stands lower is in front). Both are checked by the page and the host.
- The Office view has a **Fullscreen** mode (a button under **Fit**, or `f`; Esc leaves): only the office shows — no dashboard header, tabs or bars — with the zoom buttons, the overview (not on a phone), a compact office switcher and Exit floating over it. It uses the browser's fullscreen; where a page cannot have it (iPhone Safari) or the browser refuses, the office covers the window instead, clear of the safe areas. Pan, zoom, hover, click-to-chat (which leaves fullscreen first) and live updates keep working; nothing about it is remembered.
