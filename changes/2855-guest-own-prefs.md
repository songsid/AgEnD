- **Guests keep their own sidebar and look (#2855).** A guest on the web dashboard can now star agents, sort the list (Custom or Recent replies) and filter it, and pick their theme and language. These are saved on the host under their own account, so they are the same on every device they sign in from, the public link included. Reply times on a guest's list count only replies their agent's channel showed. Guests still can't drag to reorder, and nothing they choose changes anyone else's dashboard. Removing a guest in Settings → Guests also deletes what they saved.
