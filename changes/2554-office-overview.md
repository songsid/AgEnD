- The Office view's switcher gains **All offices** once two or more offices are stored (remembered per device like any office pick): every stored office as a tile on one canvas, each drawn exactly as its own view — its style (Desks, Studio or an uploaded skin pack, each pack loaded once and shared), its members, seats, poses, bubbles, live status, walking and in-office traffic — scaled into the tile and labelled with its name and head count. Instances in no office get a **Lobby** tile (a toggle, on by default; disabled, saying so, when everyone is in an office). Each tile is as large as its office's drawn content (a zones scene 16:9, a Desks office just its rooms, never enlarged), packed on rows in stored order so the whole overview shows as large as the panel allows; tile titles are page labels at a readable size, and below about 75% a tile's name tags show on hover only. The canvas controls are the same (pan, zoom, **Fit**/**Fill**, the overview, fullscreen; the view is kept for All offices on its own); hover, a click for the profile card and a double-click on a person for their chat work across tiles, a tile's label (or a double-click on the tile) opens that office, and traffic between people in different offices flies straight between their seats. Tiles off screen are not drawn. **Edit offices** and **Edit layout** wait until a single office is shown.
