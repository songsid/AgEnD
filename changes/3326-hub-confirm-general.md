section: Fixed
---
- **Hub settings can be confirmed in General.** On a fleet with Discord or Telegram, changing Hub membership, retention, the browser certificate, sharing an agent or a pairing step now sends its confirm card to General, the same way General's location does. Before, no card was ever sent, although the page said to confirm in General.
- **A change no General can confirm says why.** When no General can take a confirm card, the waiting card now says why in plain words (no chat app connected, no General, its chat not connected, no topic yet, a change spanning platforms, or the card could not be posted), with a link to the place that fixes it. The host command is only shown folded under Advanced.
