---
section: Changed
---
- **The web dashboard keeps you signed in for two days by default (#1670).** A sign-in on the dashboard reached directly (this computer, a LAN, a tailnet) now lasts 48 hours, and also 48 hours without use, instead of 12 hours / 2 hours idle. A public-link session stays at 4 hours / 30 minutes. A value you set in `web.session.local.*` or **Settings → Web** still wins, and the limits are still 1 to 720 hours. Sessions signed in before the update keep their original 12-hour end; they just no longer time out after 2 hours idle. Setting only a shorter session length (for example `absolute_hours: 24`) is still valid: the idle time follows it. Only an idle time above 48 hours without a session length is refused.
