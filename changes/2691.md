- Office: a seat can be reserved for one or more instances, so poses drawn for a particular seat, such as two people leaning towards each other on a sofa or someone at their own desk, always come out right. In **Edit layout** a selected seat has **Reserved for** (the office's members, several allowed) and **Lend when away**, and a reserved seat's handle is ringed. A pack can reserve seats too: a seat may be `{ "x": …, "y": …, "for": ["<instance>", …], "lend": false }` beside `[x, y]`. Whoever a seat is for takes it first when they come to its area. Nobody else sits there unless it is lent while they are all elsewhere, and a borrower moves off when one of them comes. Plain seats work as before. A reserved seat that is not lent, whose people are all elsewhere, does not count towards an area's room for the crowd. The office's reservations are saved as `seatFor`, and on disk the host keeps them beside the offices as it does night places, so a host rolled back to an earlier 2.3.2 beta still reads every office; only the reservations are missing there.
