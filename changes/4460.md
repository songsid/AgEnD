---
section: Changed
---
- **A creator may set a new agent's tool profile up to its own (#4460).** `create_instance` and `deploy_template` accept `tool_set` at or below the caller's own profile. `full` and `general` cannot be granted that way. Over the cap is refused, and the message names the cap. There is no confirmation card. A new agent starts on that profile. Changing an existing agent's `tool_set` through `update_instance_config` stays General-only. `skipPermissions` stays refused. General may still set a writable profile and still cannot change its own.
