section: Added
---
- Office skin packs can now customize the Agent profile card via an optional `card` manifest key (#4256): choose a curated preset (`default`, `rpg`, `compact`, or `dossier`), override colours with strict WCAG-checked `#rrggbb` values, attach a lazy-loaded `backgroundImage` (up to 1024×1024 px with a readability scrim) or 9-slice `frame` (up to 512×512 px), and configure avatar size/source (`sprite` or `portrait`), header alignment, desktop width, section/row order, columns, and hideable sub-fields. Packs without `card` remain unchanged and share-link privacy stripping is preserved.
