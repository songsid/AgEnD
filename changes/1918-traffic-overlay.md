- **Web: the org canvas shows live traffic between instances (#1918).** Messages sent with `delegate_task`, `send_to_instance`, `report_result`, `request_information` and `broadcast` are drawn as coloured lines from sender to receiver, one colour per kind (a legend explains them).
  - **What a line shows:** dotted while queued, a moving dash while it's being delivered, fading for three seconds once delivered. It's gone once read. A failed or uncertain delivery leaves the canvas; Needs you shows it.
  - **Routing:** lines run in the space between cards and never cross one. Lines that share a route show ×N.
  - **Zoomed out:** lines combine into one per pair of teams.
  - **Broadcasts:** a broadcast fans out from its sender. To more than eight instances it becomes one line per receiving team, ×N.
  - **Turning it off:** a Live switch turns the overlay off on this device.
  - **The list:** says the same in words ("Sending: delegate_task → quill").
  - **The public link** has no live stream, so it refreshes the picture every few seconds while the page is shown.
  - **What it never shows:** message text, or any instance whose `cross_instance_visibility` is `hidden`.
