- Office skin packs may use WebP pictures (`.webp`) as well as PNG, checked and stripped of metadata the same way, and a picture may now be up to 5120 px a side (still 16 Mpx and 4 MiB a file). A 5120×2880 background now fits at full size: about 3.2 MB as WebP against about 23.8 MB as PNG.
