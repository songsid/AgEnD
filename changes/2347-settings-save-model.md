section: Changed
---
- **Web Settings: one save model — an edit is unsaved until you press Save (#2347).** In General, Web and Web authority, changing a field no longer needs **Review changes**: it is unsaved at once, marked with a straight bar on its left, and the bar at the bottom says **N unsaved change(s)**, what saving costs, **Discard** and **Save**. Dialogs end with **Done**. A value that is not valid keeps Save waiting (**Fix 1 field(s) to save**) instead of quietly dropping your other edits. Unsaved edits stay while you move between Settings tabs.
