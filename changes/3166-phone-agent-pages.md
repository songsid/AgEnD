- Phones: an agent's View, Log and Details pages now have the same short header as its chat, "← name ● ⋯", with no row of tabs under it. ⋯ opens the same sheet as in the chat. Its first tile is **Chat**, followed by the agent's other pages, **Search** and **Usage**, then **Text size**, **Agent details** and whatever controls the page has (on Details: restart, stop, pause and its appearance). Desktop is unchanged.
