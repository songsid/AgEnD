- **Hub: group permissions and a welcome page (experimental, off by default; #3298).** On a Hub with group permissions, the Hub administrator gets Administration → Group permissions. There they choose directory groups and what their members may do (chat with agents, share agents, connect a fleet), see what a change does before saving, and resume paused items. Signed-in people get a welcome with two entries, Chat with an agent and Connect your fleet, shaped by what they may do. A Hub host's fleet Settings → Hub links to the editor. Nothing of this appears until the Hub's enforcement is installed.
