- Office skin packs can name where letters from other offices land with `"mailDrop": [x, y]`, such as a mailbox or a counter. It's checked like `door`, inside the canvas. Later letters wait 16 px to its right, and none lands on a seat. Edit layout shows it as an envelope you can drag, so one office can have its own spot (`overrides.mailDrop`). On a pack without one, the envelope is dashed where letters land today, and letters still land at the waypoint nearest the middle.
