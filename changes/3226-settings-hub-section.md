- **Settings → Hub.** Hub settings have their own section in Settings, and searching for "hub" finds them. The membership switch, Connect to a Hub (address and pairing code, fingerprint comparison, approval steps, then paired), the agents shared there, and, on a fleet that runs a Hub, its retention and browser certificate cards now live together there instead of under Access & security. Access & security links to it. The Chinese wording now says Fleet and Agent, as the rest of Settings does.
