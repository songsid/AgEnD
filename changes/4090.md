section: Changed
---
- You can zoom and pan a picture while marking it up for an agent. On a desktop, use the mouse wheel or the − / % / + buttons (click the % to fit the whole picture); hold Space, use the middle button or turn on ✋ to drag it. On a phone, pinch with two fingers to zoom and move it; one finger still draws. Marks stay where you drew them, and the picture sent to the agent is the same at any zoom. Before, the picture stayed fitted, and on a phone a pinch drew a stray line.
