- **Settings on a phone: a list of sections, each opening on its own (#2347).** On a phone, Settings opens on the list of its sections, with search on top and the "You" and "Fleet" groups as full-width rows. A section opens on its own page with "‹ Settings" to go back. This replaces the row of sections that scrolled sideways and was cut off at both edges. Unsaved changes stay in the Save bar above the bottom tabs as you move between the list and a section. Wider screens keep the list beside the section, as before.
