- Office: **Drag and pat** (per device, on by default, off with reduced motion). Pick someone up and drop them anywhere (on a phone, press and hold), and they walk back to their seat. Their card has **Pat** and **Feed**, which show a few hearts and a speech bubble. It's only on your own screen unless you tick **Tell <agent>** on their card (per agent, off by default): then the agent's next message carries one `[Office] … patted your head ×N` line, without waking it. A skin pack can set the words with `bubbles.<lang>.pat` and `.feed`.
