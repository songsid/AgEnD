- **Web: one Fleet entry, and four tabs on a phone (#2183).** The sidebar listed both **Fleet overview** and **Fleet**, and also **View**. Now:
  - the sidebar's **Fleet** row (which opened the open agent's Details) and its **View** row are gone. An agent's View, Log and Details are the tabs under its header;
  - **Fleet overview** is called **Fleet** in English (still "Fleet 概覽" in Chinese). It opens the Fleet tab you used last on this device, or the Org chart the first time;
  - a phone's bottom tabs are **Chat · Needs you · Fleet · Settings**. **Chat** is the current tab on any of an agent's pages and goes to that agent's chat;
  - someone reading `/view` without signing in sees the same sidebar and tabs as before.
