section: Added
---
- Office skin packs have three new optional fields. A seat's `standFacing` (`up`, `down`, `left`, `right`) makes it a standing desk: whoever works there stands facing that way instead of typing. A vehicle seat's `worker` makes it that agent's workplace: it rides there by itself while working, and nobody else takes the seat. A stop's `queue` gives each worker a place to wait off the platform; they step on once the train is in. A call (needs, crashed, stuck) still takes a worker off at once and to the Help desk. Packs without these fields are unchanged.
