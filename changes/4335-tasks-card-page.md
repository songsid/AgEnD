section: Changed
---
- **The task board is laid out like an issue tracker: a card is a wide page, with a list, one toolbar, a quick create and a phone layout.** A card opens as a page of its own (its address is `?card=`, so Back returns to the board or the list) instead of a half-width drawer: its goal, scope, definition of done and sub tasks on the left, its activity — comments, progress log and history as one timeline, comment box last — and its properties on the right (right under the title on a phone). A property is a chip that opens a menu and saves at once, and text saves when you leave the box or press Ctrl+Enter, with no Save and Cancel pair per field; a card an agent changed meanwhile still keeps your edit open and asks before it overwrites. Under a project's header, **Board | List**, **Search**, **Filter** (now also **Needs you**) and **Display** are one row; the list shows every card's priority, sorts by its column headers and folds its groups; the choices are kept on the device. **Main task** needs only a title (project, assignee and priority are optional chips; the rest is under **More options**). On a phone the board shows one status at a time and every card has a ⋯ menu with **Move to**. Boards, addresses and stored choices from earlier versions open as before; nothing in the database or the API changed.
