section: Changed
---
- **A Codex instance resumes its earlier conversation even when Codex's own list lost it (#2963).** When Codex's session database has no conversation for the instance's folder, but Codex's record of one is still there (the folder's newest conversation from the Codex CLI, with at least one turn), AgEnD now resumes it instead of starting a new one and only telling you how. The topic says so, and adds a note when the conversation is long and Codex may compact it. If Codex can't open it, the next launch starts a new conversation once, says so, and never picks that conversation automatically again.
- **`restart_instance` can resume a chosen Codex conversation (#2963).** `restart_instance { name, resume_session_id }` restarts a Codex instance into that conversation, for that launch only. The id must be one of the instance's own conversations, for its working folder, started by the Codex CLI. Otherwise nothing is restarted and the reply says why.
