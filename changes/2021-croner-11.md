- **Cron schedules no longer silently skip their first tick after a forward wall-clock step (#2021).** Upgraded `croner` from `10.0.1` to `11.0.0` (upstream fix `hexagon/croner#388` / `#393`). In `croner` 10, if the timer woke just before a scheduled occurrence and the system clock stepped forward across that instant while re-arming (for example during a WSL2 host clock resync or an NTP adjustment), the job's first occurrence was silently skipped and only recovered minutes later by the missed-tick safety net. `croner` 11 anchors the initial target occurrence before arming the timer so the first tick fires on time.
