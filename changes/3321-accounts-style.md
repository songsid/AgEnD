section: Changed
---
- **Settings → Accounts: clearer scope and recovery.** Accounts now says it lists sign-ins on this AgEnD host and on paired computers, and the host's rows have their own "On this AgEnD host" heading next to each paired computer's. If the first read fails, the section keeps its heading and offers Try again. Keep sign-in alive is a switch like the other on/off settings. The Sign in again dialog no longer repeats the setup page's heading and instructions. A keep-alive that could not reach the service now says it "will try again tomorrow".
