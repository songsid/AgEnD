- **Confirm, send back and answer task board cards from the chat (#2246, step 3).** Cards waiting for your
  confirmation, and blocked cards, are now in **Needs you**: each bot's live message in General lists its own agents'
  cards — what a card asks, or what it still lacks — with **Confirm** and **Send back** on a card to confirm and
  **Answer** on a blocked one (a fleet admin's buttons, three cards at a time). Send back and Answer ask for the words:
  reply to the message the bot posts, and your reply is the reason or the answer. A card an agent handed out is not
  repeated there — that agent tells you, including when it waits for your confirmation — and the web's Needs you list
  opens each card. Pressing Send back or Answer again while its prompt waits says so instead of posting another. Over the public link, Needs you leaves out the cards the board hides there. See [features](features.md#on-the-dashboard-fleet--tasks).
