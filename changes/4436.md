section: Changed
---
- General can now get a held agent-to-agent message approved for you. Tell it the short ticket id you see (for example R0a1b2c3d) and it proposes the decision; you still confirm on the card. It can also show what is held right now and who must decide, along with standing rules, ClassicBot room modes and agents held by a retained origin. When it can't propose something, it says why and what to do next instead of an error code.
