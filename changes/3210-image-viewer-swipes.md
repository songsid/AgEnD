- Phone image viewer: swipe a picture left or right to see the next or previous picture in the chat, and swipe it down (or up) to close the viewer. The picture follows your finger and the dark background fades as you pull. A short swipe springs back. When you have zoomed in, a swipe moves around the picture instead.
- Image viewer: previous and next (the arrows, and the ← and → keys on a computer) now go through every picture in the chat in message order, not only the pictures of one message. They stop at the first and the last picture instead of starting over.
