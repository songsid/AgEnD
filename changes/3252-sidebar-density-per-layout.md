---
section: Changed
---
- The agent list's **Detailed / Compact** setting is now kept separately for the phone's drawer and the desktop's sidebar, so changing one no longer changes the other. The list's **Custom** menu and **Settings → This device** show and change the setting for the screen you're on. The new defaults are **Compact** on a desktop and **Detailed** on a phone. If you had already chosen a setting, the desktop keeps it, and the phone starts at Detailed until you choose there. Both settings still follow you across devices.
