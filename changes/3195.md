section: Fixed
---
- **The `task` tool no longer reports success for a field it ignores.** A field the action does not take, such as `project` on an update, used to be dropped silently while the call reported success. Now the call is refused by name and nothing is changed, for example `update does not take "project" … Nothing was changed.`, followed by the fields the action does take. A plain update's answer also shows the saved value of each field it set.
