- **The task board: projects, main and sub tasks, and your confirmation (#2246).** Fleet › Tasks is now a board.
  It opens on your projects, each with how far along it is, and on what waits for you: finished work to **Confirm** or
  **Send back**, blocked cards to **Answer**. A project is a board of its main tasks by status — drag a card to move
  it (or Alt+arrows on its title; **Position** in the card on a phone), filter by assignee — and each main task opens
  up its sub tasks. A card opens with every field editable, its comments, progress log and history; an edit or a move
  that started before an instance's newer write is refused rather than overwriting it, and what you typed is kept. A
  main task opened on the dashboard stops for your confirmation when it is finished, and only a person signed in on the
  dashboard confirms it: an instance's `task` `done` on such a card moves it to `review`, and the web token alone is
  held to an instance's rules. Over the public link the board is read-only. Instances work the board through the
  `task` tool: `task mine` lists their work (what you sent back first, with your reason), `task move` moves a card,
  `task get M-3` shows a card with the comments new to them, and every write tells them what the board has to say.
  Tasks from before keep their ids, under **No project**; retention keeps an active project's finished cards (raise
  `retention_days` before rolling back to a release without the board). See
  [features](features.md#on-the-dashboard-fleet--tasks).
