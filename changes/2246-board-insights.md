- **Insights on the task board.** A project's **Insights** counts its cards: per assignee (each instance's load, the most cards first), status, priority or size, each row a bar split by status, priority or size, with the numbers. Count every card with the sub tasks, or main tasks alone; it counts what the board shows. See [features](features.md#on-the-dashboard-fleet--tasks).
