section: Added

- Office skin packs can now define optional custom `snacks` (1–12 items with `sprite`, optional `eat` overlay, localized `label`, and attraction `radius`) and character `eat` animations (`characters.<set>.eat`): when **Drag and pat** is enabled on a single zones office, viewers can drag a snack (or tap to select) onto an awake character to feed them or drop it on empty floor so up to 4 nearby idle characters race to claim and eat it while losing racers scatter back to their seats (respecting **Idle people stay in their own area**). Packs without `snacks` stay unchanged (#4255).
