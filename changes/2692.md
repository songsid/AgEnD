- **Agents can check, upload, replace, delete and restore the Office view's skin packs, behind a switch that is off unless a person turns it on.** With **Office skin packs** on for an agent (Dashboard access; `office_pack_access: true`, its own switch beside `office_access`), it has `office_pack_check` (the host's own checks, nothing stored: `PACK OK` or the first refusal), `office_pack_upload` (the web upload's checks and limits; an id the fleet keeps is replaced at once — checked and written first, then swapped in, the old one kept on any failure — and its previous version is kept), `office_pack_delete`, `office_pack_restore` (back to the kept previous version), `office_pack_list` and `office_set_style`. A pack is a folder in the agent's working directory or the fleet's shared folder, read on the agent's side (realpath, only the files its manifest names). An upload that would leave a stored office's layout overrides invalid is refused, naming the office and the rule, unless `drop_invalid_overrides: true` drops exactly those entries (a seat's reservation one seat at a time, never an office's whole layout). Every change is logged with the manifest's and each picture's sha256 and told to the admins in General; on the Office page (Edit offices → Agents changing packs) the admins choose **Need my yes** (the default: a change waits until approved there or from the notice's buttons) or **Apply at once**. At most 20 batches and 200 MiB an hour per agent (#2692).
