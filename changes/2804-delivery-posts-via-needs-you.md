- **Delivery problems reach you only through "Needs you" (#2804).** General no longer gets separate "❌ not delivered", "⚠️ could not be confirmed", "N queued deliveries expired" or "still waiting for its delivery lane" posts. A failed or unconfirmed delivery, including one that expired in the queue, is a "Needs you" item with its **Acknowledge** button, as before; the sending agent still gets its own notice. The details stay in the log.
