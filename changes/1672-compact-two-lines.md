---
section: Changed
---
- **Web chat: a message between agents takes two lines.** A cross-instance line ("A → B:" and its message), or any other muted status line, that is longer than two lines now folds to exactly two: the header and the first line, each on one row with an ellipsis. The fade and the separate "Show all N lines" row are gone. A small "All N lines" control sits at the end of line 2. Clicking it, or clicking the text, opens the whole message, and "Show less" folds it again. The control is announced as "Show all N lines" with its expanded state.
