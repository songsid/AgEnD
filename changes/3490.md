- **Hub group permissions work with Active Directory when the directory base is the domain root (#3490).** On such a Hub, adding a group failed with "The directory didn't answer", and once group permissions were on, every page said it couldn't confirm right now, although signing in still worked. Adding a group now works there. When a directory lookup does fail, the page says which step failed (the server couldn't be reached, its certificate wasn't trusted, the Hub's service account was refused, or the lookup didn't finish) and what to try, and the Hub's log records the step. Saving group permissions turned on with no group listed now says that nobody will get access through a group, and asks once.
