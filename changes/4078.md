section: Changed
---
- **A phone held sideways uses the phone layout (#4078).** A touch screen in a short window, as wide as a phone sideways, now gets the ☰ menu drawer, the phone's headers and text sizes, and the phone's own settings (such as the agent list's density) instead of the desktop's sidebar and headers. Tablets and computers with a mouse are unchanged, and a tablet's on-screen keyboard never switches it.
