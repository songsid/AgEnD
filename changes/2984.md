section: Fixed

- Windows Claude pane agents no longer repeatedly resume a conversation that was never saved. A runner-confirmed missing transcript starts one fresh conversation with a notice; uncertain evidence appears in Needs-you with an administrator's “Start a new conversation” action. Previous session IDs and input receipts are kept. Temporary host outages keep normal retries; a stale administrator decision cannot replace a conversation that is readable again.
- Busy Windows pane delivery now re-observes and retries only an Enter that the runner positively refused without sending. Retries are bounded; unknown outcomes never replay input, and exhausted deliveries remain uncertain in Needs-you.
- Native Windows delivery follows the ordinary-pane recognised-runtime-dialog policy, so a busy or resumed Codex layout is no longer rejected solely by a composer reader. Composer-only operations and human card answers keep their separate checks.
