- **A comment on a task card says where it stands, and the Tasks page tells you what is new for you (#4335).** Under a comment written for an agent, a line per agent says how far it got — "Sent to dev" → "dev read it" → "dev is working…" → "dev replied" (a link to the reply) — or why nothing started: the agent is offline or paused, its task board is off, the comment was refused (loop guard, no such agent, comment wakes off) or the card no longer waits on it — also when you edited a comment whose notice was still waiting and the replacement could not be sent, instead of reading "withdrawn". "Working", offline and paused are read from the agent's state, the same as the card's "Running" mark, and how far a notice got comes from the delivery outbox; a line that settled well is dropped from a comment older than a day, a failure is always shown. For you, a number on the sidebar's Fleet row, a dot on a card's tile and row, a strip above the board and on Home, and a "New" tag on a comment say that an agent answered a comment of yours, mentioned you, or handed you a card to confirm or answer; opening the card marks it read. Only cards that are not cancelled and comments of the last 30 days count, and a person who never opened the page starts with nothing unread; what you have read is kept under your own key, so two accounts that are both named alike do not share it. A read-only session sees neither. Your unread is worked out again only when the board changes or you open a card, not on every delivery update, so an open page costs the fleet almost nothing while nothing happens. Nothing is rewritten and no data is lost on a downgrade: `task_entries.wakes`, `task_card_reads`, `task_reads_floor` and an index on the comments' time are additive and old code never reads them.
