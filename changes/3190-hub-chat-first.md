section: Changed

- Hub (preview): **Chats** now opens your most recent room or chat. With nothing to open, and on **New chat**, it shows one list of the agents you can talk to: shared with you, the Hub assistant, and the agents in your rooms. Your fleets moved to **My shared agents → My fleets**, where revoking a fleet is in its row's ⋯ menu and the steps to connect another fleet stay folded once you have one. The Hub's name now sits under "AgEnD Hub" in the sidebar, and **New chat** and **New room** head the conversation list.
