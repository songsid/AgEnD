- **A Trusted level for web guests: a read-only View and search (#2855).** In Settings → Access & security → Guests each person is now **Chat** (the default, as before) or **Trusted** (`web.guests.grants.<account>.level: trusted`). A Trusted guest can also watch its agents' screens, read-only (no typing or keys; a line says it is watching only), and search the chat messages it can already see (its channel's, or its own; at most 20 results; no messages between agents, tasks, teams or settings). Making someone Trusted waits for a fleet admin's confirmation in General, like adding an agent; back to Chat applies at once, and both are in the section's Recent changes. Limits per guest: 2 screen reads a second and 2 agents at once, 6 searches a minute and one at a time; every guest watching an agent shares one capture of it per 500 ms, and at most 3 guest searches run at once. On the dashboard's own port a guest's screen read goes through these checks too.
