- Hub administrators can create Teams with a department and lead; leads manage selected members through My teams. Search covers the whole company and shows each person's sign-in name. Team membership is live for ask grants and room-creation rights, and a snapshot for room members and agent invitations. Deleting a Team ends future Team-based permissions while keeping explicit room membership and accepted agent consent. New person, group and Team grants are capped at 90 days; existing longer grants keep their recorded expiry, and expiry clocks continue during sharing pauses.
